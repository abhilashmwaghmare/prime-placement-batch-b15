## 💡 Study Strategies

### Method 1: Spaced Repetition
1. Read scenario
2. Wait 24 hours
3. Review scenario (without notes)
4. Wait 1 week
5. Practice explaining out loud
6. Move to next scenario

### Method 2: Problem-Solving Approach
1. Read scenario (problem description only)
2. Pause and think of solution
3. Compare with suggested solution
4. Understand why your approach differs
5. Memorize key learnings

### Method 3: Teaching Others
1. Read scenario thoroughly
2. Explain it to a friend/mentor
3. Record yourself explaining
4. Watch recording and improve
5. Teach another scenario

### Method 4: Interview Simulation
1. Have friend/AI ask about scenario
2. Explain your troubleshooting approach
3. Answer follow-up questions
4. Get feedback
5. Improve weak areas

## 🎯 Success Tips

### Study Tips
1. **Consistency** - 1 hour/day beats 7 hours on Sunday
2. **Active recall** - Close doc and explain scenarios
3. **Spaced repetition** - Review at increasing intervals
4. **Teaching** - Teach concepts to someone else
5. **Feedback** - Record yourself and improve

### Interview Tips
1. **Think out loud** - Explain your process
2. **Ask questions** - Clarify ambiguous requirements
3. **Mention tools** - Reference specific commands
4. **Give examples** - Use real scenarios you've faced
5. **Stay calm** - Breathe, take your time
6. **Admit uncertainty** - "I'm not sure, but I would..."
7. **Follow up** - Ask what they'd like to know more about

### Time Management
- **Week 1**: Easy scenarios + study plan
- **Week 2**: Medium scenarios + practice explaining
- **Week 3**: Hard scenarios + decision trees
- **Week 4**: Mock interviews + refinement
