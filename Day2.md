### 2. Resume Headline / Professional Summary

**Guidelines:**
- ✅ 250 characters maximum (2-4 lines)
- ✅ Should highlight your experience and key skills
- ✅ Tailor to target role

**Example Headlines:**

**Example 1:**
> DevOps Engineer with experience in building, automating, and maintaining cloud-native infrastructure and CI/CD pipelines. Proven ability to manage containerized workloads, infrastructure as code, and production-grade monitoring. Hands-on involvement in high-availability systems, release automation, and reliability improvements across development and operations teams.

**Example 2:**
> DevOps Engineer with hands-on experience in cloud infrastructure, CI/CD automation, and container orchestration. Strong focus on reliability, scalability, and secure deployment practices across production environments. Experienced in supporting high-availability systems and continuous delivery pipelines.

**Example 3:**
> Cloud and DevOps Engineer specializing in infrastructure automation, container platforms, and deployment pipelines. Actively involved in building resilient systems, streamlining release workflows, and maintaining operational stability in cloud-native environments.

**Example 4:**
> DevOps Engineer with experience in infrastructure monitoring, logging, and performance optimization. Skilled in maintaining production systems, automating operational tasks, and supporting incident response in cloud-based environments.

## ✅ Resume Do's and Don'ts

### ✅ DO's Checklist

- [ ] Remove education section if totally irrelevant
- [ ] Use action verbs in roles and responsibilities
- [ ] Quantify achievements where possible
- [ ] Keep it concise (1-2 pages)
- [ ] Use consistent formatting
- [ ] Tailor resume to job description
- [ ] Proofread multiple times
- [ ] Save in PDF format

**Additional Do's:**
- [ ] Use professional email (outlook.com recommended)
- [ ] Include relevant keywords from JD
- [ ] Highlight achievements over duties
- [ ] Use bullet points for readability
- [ ] Keep contact information updated

---

### ❌ DON'Ts Checklist

**Avoid These Common Mistakes:**
- [ ] ❌ Don't use "I can", "I will", "I would" statements
- [ ] ❌ Don't explain technologies in detail
- [ ] ❌ Don't use client names or project names
- [ ] ❌ Don't use "know", "learnt", "knowledge" type words in Roles and Responsibilities
- [ ] ❌ Don't use AI-generated content (write original)
- [ ] ❌ Don't include irrelevant information
- [ ] ❌ Don't use unprofessional email addresses
- [ ] ❌ Don't exceed 2 pages (unless 10+ years experience)
- [ ] ❌ Don't use fancy fonts or colors
- [ ] ❌ Don't include photos (unless required in your region)

**Self-Check:**
- [ ] Reviewed entire resume for "I" statements
- [ ] Removed all client/project names
- [ ] Removed technology explanations
- [ ] Replaced "know/learnt" with action verbs
- [ ] Ensured original content (no AI)
